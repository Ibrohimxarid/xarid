### Royal Air Force Museum (London & Midlands/Cosford) (London / Cosford (Shropshire), United Kingdom)

1. **Museum:** Royal Air Force Museum (London & Midlands/Cosford) (London / Cosford (Shropshire), United Kingdom)
2. **Contact:** Collections Division (collections enquiries), askcollections@rafmuseum.org, https://www.rafmuseum.org.uk/research/research-enquiries/contact-collections-division.aspx; Collections Development Committee (Objects Transfer Programme), https://www.rafmuseum.org.uk/research/default/acquisitions-and-transfers/object-transfer-programme/
3. **Reason for contact:** The RAF Museum is transferring aircraft from its national collection; several (SR.53, H.126) were still awaiting new homes.
4. **Exhibits of interest:** Saunders-Roe SR.53 XD145, Hunting H.126 XN714, and other research/test aircraft or engines in current transfer batches
5. **Why it may suit Tashkent Polytechnic Museum:** Landmark British aviation engineering; would anchor an aviation section at TPM.
6. **Source:** https://crm-media.museumsassociation.org/Transfers-from-the-Royal-Air-Force-Museum-LT_Feb-2026.pdf
7. **Suggested next step:** Email the Collections Development Committee now — ask which aircraft from the 2025/2026 batches were not taken by UK bodies (SR.53, H.126) and whether a foreign public museum can apply under "alternative routes".

**To:** askcollections@rafmuseum.org  
**Subject:** Potential transfer of retired museum exhibits to Tashkent Polytechnic Museum

Dear Colleagues,

I am writing on behalf of Tashkent Polytechnic Museum in Tashkent, Uzbekistan — a museum of science, engineering, transport and the history of technology. We are currently developing a modern, hands-on exhibition for school students, families and young engineers.

We understand from your published information that the Museum's Objects Transfer Programme is offering aircraft from the national collection, and that the Saunders-Roe SR.53 and Hunting H.126 were still awaiting new homes.

We would be very interested to learn whether these items might be available for transfer, donation or purchase. Our particular interest is in: Saunders-Roe SR.53 XD145, Hunting H.126 XN714, and other research/test aircraft or engines in current transfer batches.

If any items could be considered, we would be grateful for basic information on their condition, dimensions and weight, available documentation, and any conditions or procedures for transfer to another institution. We are ready to discuss the practical arrangements for dismantling, packing and international transport, and we would gladly acknowledge Royal Air Force Museum (London & Midlands/Cosford) as the source of the exhibits.

We fully understand that any decision depends on your own collection policies and priorities, and we would be happy to follow whatever process you have in place.

Thank you very much for considering our request.
