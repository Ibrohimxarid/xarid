Dear Colleagues,

I am writing on behalf of Tashkent Polytechnic Museum in Tashkent, Uzbekistan — a museum of science, engineering, transport and the history of technology. We are currently developing a modern, hands-on exhibition for school students, families and young engineers.

We understand from your published information that the Spoorwegmuseum is deaccessioning part of its rolling stock following its collection review, first offering it to other museums and heritage railways.

We would be very interested to learn whether these items might be available for transfer, donation or purchase. Our particular interest is in: Surplus locomotives, carriages, bogies and railway components from the rolling-stock review.

If any items could be considered, we would be grateful for basic information on their condition, dimensions and weight, available documentation, and any conditions or procedures for transfer to another institution. We are ready to discuss the practical arrangements for dismantling, packing and international transport, and we would gladly acknowledge Het Spoorwegmuseum (Dutch Railway Museum) as the source of the exhibits.

We fully understand that any decision depends on your own collection policies and priorities, and we would be happy to follow whatever process you have in place.
